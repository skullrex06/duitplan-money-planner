# DuitPlan — Personal Money Planner

**Plan Your Money. Control Your Future.**

DuitPlan is a simple, mobile-friendly personal finance planner that helps you record income, track expenses, manage monthly budgets, monitor debts and build savings goals — all from one place.

Designed for people who want a clearer picture of their money without complicated spreadsheets or connecting their bank accounts.

---

## Why DuitPlan?

Managing money should not feel complicated. DuitPlan gives you a practical place to record your daily finances and review where your money goes.

- **Know your money:** See your income, expenses and cash flow in one dashboard.
- **Stay within budget:** Set monthly spending limits and monitor category totals.
- **Track debt:** Record outstanding balances, repayment amounts and payment history.
- **Build savings:** Set savings targets and record each contribution.
- **Keep your data private:** Your finance records are stored locally on your device.
- **Use your language:** English, Bahasa Melayu, Simplified Chinese and Tamil.

## Privacy, Storage & Important Information

DuitPlan is designed for personal record-keeping.

- Data is stored in local browser/app storage on the device being used.
- DuitPlan does **not** connect to bank accounts or automatically import transactions.
- DuitPlan does **not** transfer, hold or process money.
- There is no cloud synchronisation between devices.
- No online account is required to use the app.
- Export backups regularly. Clearing browser or app storage, uninstalling the app, or losing the device may result in data loss.
- A profile PIN is a local access lock; it is not an online account or encrypted cloud vault.
- DuitPlan is not a bank, accounting service, tax service or provider of financial advice.

**Important:** Keep your backup file somewhere safe. The seller/developer cannot recover locally stored records if the device data is lost.

## Getting Started

1. Open DuitPlan using the app or web app link provided by the seller.
2. Create a profile and set a 4-digit PIN.
3. Select your preferred language and currency in Settings.
4. Add your financial accounts and opening balances.
5. Record income and expenses as they happen.
6. Set budgets, add debts and create savings goals.
7. Export a JSON backup regularly.

## Installation

DuitPlan is a mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA). On a supported browser:

1. Open the DuitPlan web app.
2. Open the browser menu.
3. Choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** if available.
4. Launch DuitPlan from your device's home screen.

An Android APK may also be distributed separately by the seller when a tested build is available.
